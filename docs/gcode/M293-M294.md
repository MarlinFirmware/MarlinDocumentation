---
tag: m0293
title: "M293, M294: Babystep Z Up and Down"
short_title: Babystep Z Up and Down
brief: Babystep the Z axis up or down by one step, using the emergency parser
author: thinkyhead

parent: G-code Commands
group: calibration
since: 2.1.3
requires: EP_BABYSTEPPING

codes: [ M293, M294 ]

notes: |
  - These commands need `EMERGENCY_PARSER` to be useful during a print. With `EMERGENCY_PARSER` enabled, the emergency parser handles them as they arrive, so they are not held behind queued moves.
  - Without `EMERGENCY_PARSER`, `M293` and `M294` are queued like any other G-code and run in order.
  - `M293` moves Z up and `M294` moves Z down, in the same way as `M290 Z` with a positive or negative value. Each command is one babystep increment, not a distance you set.
  - The direction follows `BABYSTEP_INVERT_Z`.
  - Babysteps are only meant for small adjustments. They do not check endstops and do not change the reported position.

examples:

- pre: Raise the nozzle one step during the first layer
  code: M293
- pre: Lower the nozzle one step
  code: M294

---

`M293` and `M294` make quick Z adjustments during a print, one step at a time. For larger adjustments, or to set a specific distance, use [`M290`](M290.html).
