notes:
- "`RESONANCE_TEST` is only offered when input shaping (`INPUT_SHAPING_X/Y/Z`) or a Fixed-Time Motion shaper is enabled, and it requires `EMERGENCY_PARSER` so the test can be stopped with `M496`."
- "`M495 S` always homes all axes first, then moves to the center of the bed at `Z_CLEARANCE_FOR_HOMING` before it starts shaking. Clear the bed first."
- "The test runs in the background: `M495 S` returns at once, and the sweep keeps going until it reaches the end frequency or `M496` stops it."
- "For the Z axis the acceleration (`A`) is capped at 15."
- "`M496` also resets every `M495` setting (axis, frequencies, octave duration, acceleration, correction) to its default."
- These settings are not saved to EEPROM.

`M495` shakes one axis back and forth while the frequency rises steadily, so that every frequency in the range gets a turn. When the sweep passes the axis's resonance frequency the printer shakes, rattles and hums noticeably harder. That frequency is the one to give the input shaper ([`M493`](/docs/gcode/M493.html) for Fixed-Time Motion, [`M593`](/docs/gcode/M593.html) for standard input shaping).

The frequency rises exponentially: it doubles every `O` seconds, so the sweep takes `O` × log<sub>2</sub>(`T` / `F`) seconds. With the defaults (5 to 100 Hz, 40 seconds per octave) that is about 173 seconds. The amplitude falls as the frequency rises, so low frequencies are not violent and high frequencies are not lost.

There are two ways to read the result:

- **With an accelerometer** on the toolhead (for X) or the bed (for Y), let the measuring tool record the whole sweep. See [Input Shaping](/docs/features/input_shaping.html#with-an-accelerometer).
- **By ear and eye**, start a stopwatch when the axis starts shaking (after homing and the move to the center), note the time when the shaking is strongest, and convert it with `M495 G<seconds>`. The LCD has the same conversion under **Motion > Resonance Test > Calc. Res. Freq.**

On printers with an LCD menu, **Motion > Resonance Test** starts the test on each axis and offers **Abort Test** while it runs.
