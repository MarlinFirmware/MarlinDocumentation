Unretract (i.e., recover, prime) the filament: unhop Z, then push back the length of the matching [`G10`](/docs/gcode/G010.html) (plain or swap, chosen automatically) plus the extra length from [`M208`](/docs/gcode/M208.html), at the `M208` feedrate.

Multiple consecutive `G11` commands without a corresponding [`G10`](/docs/gcode/G010.html) or `G10 S1` will be ignored.

"Performs two moves: An optional Z lower at the maximum Z feedrate (travel acceleration), and a recovery move at the recover feedrate (retract acceleration)."

See [Firmware Retract](/docs/features/fwretract.html#g11-recover).
